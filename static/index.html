Boardroom Twin by Lumen Works
Simulation, not a prediction

Rehearse your proposal in front of the client's board, before you send it.

A proposal team of AI agents writes the proposal. An AI copy of the client's evaluation board reviews it: executives who score it, and gatekeepers who can block it. A Bid Manager fixes what they object to, up to two rounds.

STEP 1 Proposal team drafts Business Analyst, Solution Architect, Delivery Manager, Risk & Compliance Analyst and Proposal Manager hand work to each other
STEP 2 Client board reviews 3 to 5 seats chosen from your brief. CFO, VP or Director, Enterprise Architect, Procurement and Security vote at the same time
STEP 3 Bid Manager revises Up to two rounds. A QA Reviewer blocks invented claims. The board votes again
STEP 4 Board-ready report Proposal, board decision memo, meeting prep, optional prototype

STAGE 2 OF 5

The proposal team is writing draft 1

Each agent hands its work to the next. Tools are plain code: the agent asks, code answers exactly.

    STAGE 3 OF 5 · BOARD ROUND 1

    The client's board is reviewing draft 1

    Code picked the board from the brief. Every seat reviews the draft at the same time, and code turns their answers into votes.

    Round 1 · scores

    Ran at the same time · the tick on each bar is the score needed

    Decided by code, not AI: score = sum of rating × weight. Below the needed score → Rejected. At or above it with anything still open → Yes, with a condition. Otherwise → Approved.

    Round 1 · gates

    Gatekeepers answer three checklist questions. They do not give a score.

    Decided by code, not AI: "Not met" on a must-have → Blocked. Any "Partly", or "Not met" on another item → Pass, with conditions. All "Met" → Pass.

    Lumen Works is a fictional firm. All client names and briefs are made up. Every number is decided by code, not by AI.
