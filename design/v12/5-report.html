Proposal · final version (draft 3)

Online claim status page for Northwind Insurance

Tags:FROM BRIEFCALCULATEDTO CONFIRM

1. Executive summary

A self-service page where customers check their claim status any time, cutting status calls to the contact centre. Delivered in two phases; Phase 1 alone delivers the main benefit for $12,000 CALCULATED.

2. Client situation

Customers phone to ask where their claim is FROM BRIEF

Tight budget this year; six-hour claims outage last month FROM BRIEF

3. Proposed solution

Status page reading a read-only copy of claim data, refreshed every 15 minutes. No load on the live claims system. A claim opens only with the claim number and a secure link, and the page shows status steps and the expected payment date only. One switch turns the page off within a minute.

4. Scope

In: claim lookup, status steps, expected payment date, secure link. Out: changes to the claims system, mobile app, live chat.

5. Timeline

Phase 1 build: Dec 7 – Dec 18, 2026

Holiday freeze: Dec 21 – Jan 15 (holidays Dec 25, Dec 26, Jan 1) CALCULATED

Go-live: Mon Jan 18, 2027 · Phase 2: Jan 18 – Jan 29

6. Team and cost

2 engineers · $600 per engineer-day FROM BRIEF

Phase 1: 2 × 2 weeks × 5 days × $600 = $12,000 CALCULATED

Phase 2 (optional): $12,000 · Total $24,000 ≈ €22,100

Changes: a written change request, sized in engineer-days at the same rate, agreed before work starts. Each phase is accepted against the scope list before it is invoiced.

7. Risks and mitigations

  • Load on claims system → read-only copy
  • Bad release → one-minute switch-off
  • January claim peak → go-live after the peak
  • Personal claim data → status only, secure link, access logged

8. Assumptions

Phase 2 can be funded next quarter TO CONFIRM

Claim data can be copied every 15 minutes TO CONFIRM

The client's data-retention rule applies to the read-only copy TO CONFIRM

9. Next steps

  • Confirm Phase 2 funding with Northwind's CFO
  • Confirm the data-retention rule with Northwind's security team
  • Sign Phase 1 by Dec 2 to start Dec 7
