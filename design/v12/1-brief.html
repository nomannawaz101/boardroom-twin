Rehearse your proposal in front of the client's board, before you send it.

A proposal team of AI agents writes the proposal. An AI copy of the client's evaluation board reviews it: executives who score it, and gatekeepers who can block it. A Bid Manager fixes what they object to, up to two rounds.

STEP 1Proposal team drafts: Business Analyst, Solution Architect, Delivery Manager, Risk & Compliance Analyst and Proposal Manager hand work to each other.
STEP 2Client board reviews: 3 to 5 seats chosen from your brief vote at the same time.
STEP 3Bid Manager revises: up to two rounds. A QA Reviewer blocks invented claims. The board votes again.
STEP 4Board-ready report: proposal, board decision memo, meeting prep, optional prototype.
