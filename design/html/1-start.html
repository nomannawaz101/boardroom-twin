Rehearse your proposal in front of the client's board, before you send it.

A proposal team of AI agents writes the proposal. An AI copy of the client's evaluation board reviews it: executives who score it, and gatekeepers who can block it. A Bid Manager fixes what they object to, up to two rounds.

STEP 1 Proposal team drafts Business Analyst, Solution Architect, Delivery Manager, Risk & Compliance Analyst and Proposal Manager hand work to each other
STEP 2 Client board reviews 3 to 5 seats chosen from your brief. CFO, VP or Director, Enterprise Architect, Procurement and Security vote at the same time
STEP 3 Bid Manager revises Up to two rounds. A QA Reviewer blocks invented claims. The board votes again
STEP 4 Board-ready report Proposal, board decision memo, meeting prep, optional prototype
